# Code of conduct

## The short version

This project exists because disaster recovery work is done by people under pressure, and their record of that work should belong to them. Treat the people here the way you would want to be treated on day nine of an activation.

<br>

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

<br>

## Standards

Behavior that builds this community:

- Assuming the person you are replying to has a reason you have not heard yet
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback with grace
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from it
- Focusing on what is best for people running this in the field, not for any one contributor
- Respecting that operational experience and engineering experience are both expertise, and neither outranks the other here

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

<br>

## Domain specific expectations

This project sits next to real projects, real contracts and real money.

| | |
|---|---|
| **No client or project data in issues** | Redact names, addresses, ticket numbers, contract numbers and dollar figures. Reproduce with the demo seed instead |
| **No credentials, ever** | Not in an issue, not in a screenshot, not in a log paste, not "temporarily" |
| **Disagree about the domain openly** | Practice differs by state, by county and by contract. "That is not how it works" is a useful contribution when it comes with what is actually done and where |
| **Do not use this space for procurement disputes** | Whatever happened on a program, take it up through the channels that exist for it |

<br>

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at **ryanalex314@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

### Enforcement guidelines

| Impact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Correction.** Unprofessional or unwelcome language | A private written warning explaining the violation, and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested |
| **Warning.** A single incident, or a series of actions | A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with those involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this code, for a specified period |
| **Temporary ban.** A serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behavior | A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community |
| **Permanent ban.** A pattern of violation, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward classes of individuals | A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community |
