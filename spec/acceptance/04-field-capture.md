> Each scenario restates a recorded rule; **From** names it. Values are the example world's
> (`spec/CONVENTIONS.md` §6) and the golden mission's (`spec/golden/MISSION.md` §2, §3): P1's loads
> `101001`…, haul-outs `201001`… and unit rate tickets `301001`…; P2's split load `102001-A`,
> `102001-B`. A flag's message is `spec/FLAGS.md`'s, word for word. How to read the columns:
> [README](README.md).
>
> **This file is about what is recorded.** What the worker concludes from it (pairing, statuses,
> flags, quantities, zone, mileage) is Part 6's file, written with task 1.5.1; a flag is quoted
> here only to show that the record was **taken** and the problem left for later. Who holds a
> phone, the assignment, sign-in and sync are Part 2's (`02-people-and-devices.md`); the
> certification a badge loads is Part 3's.
