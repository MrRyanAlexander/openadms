---
title: Glossary
description: Every term Open ADMS uses, in its exact meaning - from active window to zone.
---

The words below are used in exactly these meanings, across the product, the API and these pages.

| Term                          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active window**             | A project's start and end dates. A ticket outside it is flagged "project not active", judged by a load's time (its collection's end)                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Assignment**                | One person's work for one day: a project, their work (ticket types, unit rate pay items, certifying), one site where the work is at one, optionally a phone. Written on the dispatch board, or on the phone with the master passcode when there is none (an _unplanned assignment_, marked). Never expires, never stops work |
| **Audit record**              | One entry in the append-only audit log: a change's prior and new values, who, from where and why, written by the database itself                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Badge**                     | Carries the cert number; scanned at **New** to load the truck's or crew's certification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Certification**             | A one-time inspection of a **container** (measured, capacity in CY, five photographs) or a **crew** (not measured), never a vehicle. Never ends on its own; tag expiry, revocation and replacement flag later tickets                                                                                                        |
| **Cert number**               | One number: cert = placard = badge. Issued from the project's series or taken from pre-printed paper; unique per project; copied onto every field event                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Citizen drop-off log**      | A record, not a ticket, that a resident dropped debris at a site. No quantity, number or cost                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Client**                    | The jurisdiction or agency paying for cleanup, such as Bayline County. A record, reused across disasters                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Client package**            | The deliverable: a project's final tickets, photographs, receipts and transactions, assembled by hand from the exports and zipped. There is no invoice record                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Client user**               | A read-only viewer login that belongs to a client                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Completed load / haul-out** | `CLT` / `CHO`: the record the worker concludes from a ticket's field events. Not an event itself; the only load record that is QC'd and locked                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Contract**                  | A name, its prime and an optional link to the signed copy. A rule names one and runs only on its prime's tickets                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Contractor**                | A hauling company: a **prime** holds a contract; a **sub** works under one prime, at most two tiers, typed in at certification                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Control ticket**            | A pre-printed, barcoded ticket number, from ranges entered at setup. The alternative is numbers the phones make, in blocks                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Crew time**                 | A unit rate ticket listing items with hours to the quarter hour; it bills the highest item's hours, once                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Debris type**               | What a load or haul-out monitor picks on Complete, at both ends: an entry on the project's form, named as the contract names the work (Vegetative, C&D, Mulch…). A difference between the two field events is flagged                                                                                                        |
| **Declaration event**         | The disaster and its reference, such as Hurricane Delphine, `DR-9001-FL`. One shared record; many clients' projects point at it. Never shortened to "event"                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Dispatch board**            | The back office's daily plan of assignments                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Disposal site**             | Where debris legally goes: a site type (TDSR/DMS, Final Disposal, Transfer Station, Other) plus its facts (_Takes loads_, _Haul-outs leave here_, _Charges a tipping fee_, _Ineligible site_), a permit, GPS, an area and its dates                                                                                          |
| **FDS / TDSR**                | Final disposal site / temporary debris staging and reduction site (DMS means the same). Debris is reduced at a TDSR and hauled out to an FDS                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Field event**               | What one monitor records under a ticket number: collection `COL`, disposal `DIS`, haul-out collection `HOC`, haul-out disposal `HOD`, unit rate `URT`. The evidence: never edited by the monitor after submitting, corrected in place by permission                                                                          |
| **Field monitor**             | A field observer who records field events on an enrolled phone; has a system **Monitor ID** such as `M004127`                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Field tag**                 | A project list the firm fills (such as _Mixed_), read by the _Field tag_ rule criterion and exports. No phone asks for one                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Firm**                      | The organisation that installs Open ADMS and runs its own installation, usually a debris monitoring firm                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Flag**                      | How every problem is raised after setup: a plain message ("Ticket 101008: debris type mismatch. Flagged for review."), never a refusal. Fixed, or accepted with a reason                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Form**                      | What a project uses for one kind of document (load, haul-out, unit rate, truck certificate, ROE, survey, incident, drop-off log); debris types are added to it                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Haul-out**                  | The second leg: debris leaving a TDSR for final disposal, its load call taken at the loading end                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Installation**              | One firm's running copy of Open ADMS                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Invoice lock / batch**      | Locking QC Approved tickets as invoiced, optionally in a named batch. A locked ticket is unlocked, audited, before any change                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Load call**                 | The percentage of a container's certified capacity a monitor sees filled, a whole number 0–100. Billable CY = capacity × load call                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Login list**                | The sign-in checks a set-up phone holds for its project's monitors, for signing in offline                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Master passcode**           | One per firm: it enrols and sets up phones and opens the supervisor's actions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Mileage**                   | The road distance of the shortest route on public roads from the collection's end to the disposal site's own GPS, from the installation's own routing server; with no route, the straight line, flagged                                                                                                                      |
| **Pay item**                  | A priced line of the contract as set up: its code (unique on its project), its description, payable type, unit of measure, size band, capture settings and a rate for each prime. The only place a rate lives                                                                                                                |
| **Payable type**              | The work a pay item prices, from the firm's list (Vegetative, C&D, Hangers, Stumps, Crew time, Tipping fee…)                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Preload**                   | A load collected one day and dumped the next. Normal, not flagged; it counts on its collection's day                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Program type**              | The kind of work (ROW, PARKS, PPDR, WATERWAY, the firm's own), each with a _Requires ROE_ setting                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Project**                   | One body of work for one client under one declaration event: the hub everything hangs off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **QC status**                 | QC Review, QC Approved, QC Issues, QC Void: the back office's own mark, set by hand, separate from the status                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Quarantine**                | Where a sync puts a second field event of one kind on one ticket: kept in full, for a person to resolve. Never dropped                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Read-only viewer**          | A client user, a contractor user or an auditor, who reads but never changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Reconciliation**            | The worker's run: group a ticket's field events, check them, attach zone and mileage, price, write                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **ROE**                       | Right of entry: a record (number, parcel, owner, signed date, scope, photograph); on a project whose program type requires it, every collection, unit rate ticket and survey carries one                                                                                                                                     |
| **Rule**                      | The firm's pricing, per form: one contract, its criteria, and actions _Add transaction #n_, _Remove transaction #n_, _Void ticket_. All passing rules run in # order                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Scale receipt**             | The paper a final disposal site's scale house hands the tower; not a ticket. Hand-transcribed, only where the contract pays by weight                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Split load**                | One truck gathering from several properties: collections `-A`, `-B`… and one disposal on the base number; each property's share priced on its own                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Status**                    | `PENDING`, `COMPLETED`, `MANUAL_REVIEW`, `VOID`. Review still bills; only a person (with a reason) or a rule voids                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Stump**                     | A unit rate ticket: diameter two feet up (under 24 in flagged), root-ball diameter, half or more exposed; paid by the stump or by the fill                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **TBN**                       | To be negotiated: a pay item whose price isn't agreed; it bills nothing until it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Ticket**                    | A ticket number and the field events recorded under it. Paper and printouts are receipts, never records                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Time clock**                | An optional per-project add-on for workers' own hours, on their assignment; never related to crew-time billing                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Transaction**               | One priced line created by a rule: pay item, quantity, the named contract's prime's rate, prime and operating sub. Regenerated when its ticket changes                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Unit rate work**            | Work billed per each, per pound or per hour rather than per load: one monitor, one place, one ticket, no pairing                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Zone**                      | A named shape on the project's map. No monitor records it: reconciliation attaches it from the GPS                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
